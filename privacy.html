Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Annadata Fresh Mart ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you order from us.

What we collect

Your name, WhatsApp number, delivery address and order details. We collect these only to deliver your order and to contact you about it.

How we use it

To confirm and deliver orders, send order updates and the recipe kit on WhatsApp, and, if you agree, send our daily vegetable list. You can ask us to stop messages at any time by replying "STOP".

Payments

Online payments are processed by our payment partner. We do not see or store your card or UPI PIN details.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with our delivery staff and payment partner, as needed to complete your order, or when required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information by messaging us on WhatsApp.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

We want you to be happy with every order of fresh vegetables.

Cancellation

You can cancel an order free of charge before we start harvesting it: until 9 PM the previous day for a morning slot, and until 12 noon for an evening slot. Message us on WhatsApp to cancel.

Quality issues

If any item is damaged, spoiled or not as ordered, tell us on WhatsApp within 6 hours of delivery with a photo. We will replace the item in the next slot or refund its value.

Refunds

Approved refunds for online payments go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days. Cash or pay-on-delivery refunds are made by UPI.

Weight and price

Fresh produce is weighed at packing. Prices follow the market and the price shown when you order is the price you pay.

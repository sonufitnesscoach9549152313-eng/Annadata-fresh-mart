Annadata Parivar Membership – Terms

Last updated: 9 November 2026

What it is

Membership is an advance payment for vegetables from Annadata Fresh Mart. It is not an investment, deposit or loan, and it earns no interest, profit or share in the business. Your payment is only used against vegetables you order.

Plans

Saathi: pay ₹5,000, get ₹5,500 of vegetables in your account balance.
Parivar: pay ₹10,000, get ₹11,500 of vegetables, plus a free zucchini recipe kit and two farm visits a year.

How the balance works

After we confirm your UPI payment, the full balance is added to your Annadata account, usually within a few hours. Each order you pay with your balance is deducted automatically and shown in your balance history. If an order paid from the balance is cancelled, the amount is returned to your balance.

Validity

Please use your balance within 6 months of the date it is added. Vegetables come from our own farm and depend on the season's harvest.

Refunds

You can ask for your unused balance back at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp. We refund the unused part of the amount you actually paid (not the bonus value) to your UPI within 7 days. For example, if you paid ₹5,000, received ₹5,500 and used ₹2,200, the refund is ₹5,000 × (3,300 ÷ 5,500) = ₹3,000.

Limits

Membership is open to the first 100 families, so that we only promise what our farm can grow. Delivery is within Sikar only, in the 6–8 AM and 6–8 PM slots.

Your money is kept separately

Membership payments go to the business bank account and are recorded against your account. You can see every rupee added and used in "My account".
